# Analyzing-and-Escalating-a-Potential-Data-Exfiltration-Ticket-As-a-SOC-L1
The ticket has been assigned to SOC L1 for initial triage, evidence collection, and investigation.
![Image alt](https://github.com/Kevinolee1/Analyzing-and-Escalating-a-Potential-Data-Exfiltration-Ticket-As-a-SOC-L1/blob/77596a9e5e351647a8c34686df4a0982abb81a69/Screenshot%202026-08-18%20011200.png)
Sign in to your designated SOC Level 1 analyst account.
![Image alt](https://github.com/Kevinolee1/Analyzing-and-Escalating-a-Potential-Data-Exfiltration-Ticket-As-a-SOC-L1/blob/713fb73ffea99a55ad89ed1a4f20e6cfbcdd126e/Screenshot%202026-08-20%20111240.png)
Click on the Potential Data Exfiltration ticket to begin the investigation.
![Image alt](https://github.com/Kevinolee1/Analyzing-and-Escalating-a-Potential-Data-Exfiltration-Ticket-As-a-SOC-L1/blob/02756d4fc021c3029053129a508feb9c2bdf3b8a/Screenshot%202026-08-20%20111453.png)
Scroll down until you see the Post Reply section. In the response box, select Original Message. Enter your analyst actions, investigation findings, and final disposition in the text field. 
![Image alt](https://github.com/Kevinolee1/Analyzing-and-Escalating-a-Potential-Data-Exfiltration-Ticket-As-a-SOC-L1/blob/c30a629e74ecdd5318021677b0eb259ee119d72a/Screenshot%202026-08-20%20111515.png)
Scroll down and select “Post Reply” to submit your response.
![Image alt](https://github.com/Kevinolee1/Analyzing-and-Escalating-a-Potential-Data-Exfiltration-Ticket-As-a-SOC-L1/blob/a287d6a922f66c9f6cd0de8295f1a8c28995167b/Screenshot%202026-08-16%20032820.png)
Click the Internal Note tab. In the text box, document your investigation findings, analysis, and recommended next actions. Once the information has been entered and verified, click Post Note to save the internal documentation to the ticket.
![Image alt](https://github.com/Kevinolee1/Analyzing-and-Escalating-a-Potential-Data-Exfiltration-Ticket-As-a-SOC-L1/blob/8a1511df1ca7dd8edd040dbefe4666487523ba2b/Screenshot%202026-08-20%20111714.png)
Scroll back up and assign the ticket to the SOC L2 Analyst for further investigation and advanced analysis.
